Conclusion: the pump's firmware accepted and processed the 323-byte
control-frame format without error/disconnection (responded with what looks
like a legitimate protocol ACK, distinct from a read response) — so this
frame *shape* is understood by this device. But opcode1=0x01/opcode2=0x01
(MDP-20000's TURN_ON_OFF/ON) did not toggle SwitchON on this wavemaker unit.
Expected: MDP (dosing) and MOW (wavemaker) product lines almost certainly use
different opcode-to-attribute mappings for byte21/22/23/24, since their
datapoint models are entirely different (dosing channels/ml vs
flow%/frequency/mode/linkage).

Determining the correct write opcodes for THIS wavemaker requires either:
(a) a real packet capture of the Jebao Aqua app performing this exact action
    (Phase 1B) — most reliable, ground truth
(b) bounded brute-force of opcode1 values while polling status for a change
    — feasible since read-verification already works, but is a bigger/riskier
    action against real hardware than a single toggle test and needs explicit
    go-ahead before attempting
