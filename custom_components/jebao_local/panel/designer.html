Add a pump to this tank

Find a pump's did in any of its entity_ids - Developer Tools → States (filter jebao_) is fastest, or Settings → Devices & Services → Devices → the pump → click any entity. It's the part between jebao_ and the attribute name, e.g. switch.jebao_<did>_switchon. It's also shown directly in the config-flow discovery list when you first add the pump. If you have several identical pumps and can't tell which is which, the pump's MAC address is shown on its HA device page (Settings → Devices & Services → Devices → the pump, under "Connections") - cross-reference that against your router's client list to find the physical unit, then give it a memorable label below.

A tank is just a named list of pump dids, saved in this browser. Pick it from the dropdown on the left to make it the active tank for Profile, Feed, and the ON/OFF buttons.

A profile is a saved snapshot of wave mode, flow and frequency. Save one, then clone it onto every pump in the active tank with one click.
